How to Play

Blind Maze is a maze you navigate without seeing where you are.

You start in the top-left corner, facing right (east). Your goal is to reach the bottom-right corner.

Controls

  • FORWARD — move one space in the direction you're facing.
  • LEFT — turn 90° left.
  • RIGHT — turn 90° right.

Penalties

  • Boundary hit: Trying to move outside the maze counts against your boundary-hit limit.
  • Repeat wall hit: Hitting the exact same wall again counts as a repeat and brings you closer to losing.

Easy vs. Hard

  • Easy: Your facing direction is shown on the ball.
  • Hard: Your facing direction is hidden. Use the HINT button if you need a 2-second peek.

Tip: Keep track of your turns and the walls you've encountered. Since you can't see the maze, your memory is your map.
